# The edition

`the-rohonc-codex.epub` is the whole edition, free, and it is the same file the
reader at oona13.com serves. It validates clean against EPUBCheck 3.3.

Two books under one cover.

**Book One** is the translation: a reading of the gloss into continuous English,
written against the books the codex compiles, with every paragraph naming the
folios it renders. It is an interpretation and says so. 71.1% of its content
words are the manuscript's own, 9.2% come from a passage the folio was read
against, and 19.7% are English the editor supplied. `harness/ktverify.py risk`
is that accounting and ranks every paragraph by how much of it came from
somewhere other than the manuscript.

**Book Two** is the evidence: all 441 written pages in manuscript order, line
for line, every word marked for how well it is known. Where the two books
disagree, Book Two is right.

The print edition is 6x9, 553 pages. The print PDF is not kept here.
