## 004v

In the beginning was the Lord God ... and the earth. The sun and the moon are
written. The angel of God spoke to Elijah the prophet: before the making of man
there was the forefather. Father Adam bowed down before the Lord God, eternal in
heaven, and before the brethren. There were many angels, and God the Father had
many angels about him. Among the angels, two hundred and fifty-seven years before
the making of man, were Father Adam and the angels; and there was Lucifer, and two
angels with him. And the angel Lucifer prayed, forty days and forty nights. (004v)

## 004r

The angel of the Father spoke to God the Father, and the angel spoke to Lucifer,
because of the cup that was hidden away above, with the Father, on the throne. And
then God, and the angel, and Lucifer: the cup was hidden away above the Lord on the
throne, and the cup was [stolen] and hidden away above. And when Lucifer had done
it, the angel went back to the Father. And then the angel, and the Lord, and the
cup: Lucifer [stole] the cup and hid it away above, and the two of them went. God
the Father, and the angel of the Father, spoke to Lucifer; and Lucifer said: because
the cup was hidden away above, with the Father, on the throne. And when Lucifer went
to God, the angel said: God, and the angel, and the cup hidden away above the Lord
on the throne. And the cup of Lucifer ... the cup was hidden away ... and then it
was hidden. The angel went back to God the Father. (004r)

## 002r

The mother of the Lord God was born; and Lucifer spoke to the Lord, to the Lord;
Lucifer set himself upon the throne, equal to the Lord God. There was the earth;
and then God the Father eternal, and Michael. The angels, faithful servants, rose
up; and when they had risen the heavenly ones spoke. God the Father eternal went to
Lucifer, and Lucifer bowed down before the throne of the Lord; and within, they
bowed down, every one of the angels, to him whom Lucifer would not bow to. And then
God the Father eternal saw it, and cried out: Lucifer has gone from the commandment,
because every angel bowed to him. And he refused, said God the Father, and he
departed. This was the angel. He left the food, and three times the angel said to
Elijah the prophet: Elijah, say that God the Father is. (002r)

## 002v

The Son, the Spirit, the breath of life; and then the Son in the image of the Lord.
The breath and the likeness are in man, all one, to the Father and to the Son. The
Father, the Son and the Spirit took man, and every living creature. The soul hears,
man sees, living rightly; not many from the Father, not many from the Son, not many
of the Holy Spirit, but this Lord is all, one God. And then God, and the angel, and
Elijah the prophet: Elijah the prophet, when the Father and the Son and the Spirit
went out into this world, and into Paradise. The Lord God made man from the slime of
the earth; and then man was, as was said before, slime of the earth, and became a
living soul. He breathed into Adam, and Adam lived; and the Lord God took man, and
man went into the Garden of Eden. End of chapter. (002v)
