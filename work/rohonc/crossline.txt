  Above chance means the text carries on over the margin. At chance
  means the line is composed as a unit.

  `lift` is the ratio to chance and is the wrong way to compare texts
  here: the Rohonc's chance level is already 77% at n=2, so its ratio
  cannot exceed 1.30 however continuous the text is, while a text with
  a 6% chance level has room for 17x. `headroom` is the share of the
  distance from chance to 100% that the text actually covers, which is
  comparable across manuscripts with very different repeat rates.
