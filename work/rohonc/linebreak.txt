================================================================================================
DOES TOKEN-TO-TOKEN DEPENDENCE SURVIVE THE LINE BREAK?
================================================================================================
Surrogate-corrected mutual information between one token and the next,
in bits. `matched` is the within-line figure recomputed on a random
subsample the same size as the across-break set, so the two rest on
equal evidence. `keep` is across as a percentage of matched.

                               within   matched   across      sd   sigma  keep % n across
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rohonc (as stored)             1.4222    1.0495   0.2391  0.0142    16.9    22.8     4036
Rohonc (line reversed)         1.4211    1.0472   0.0346  0.0156     2.2     3.3     4036
Voynich EVA (words)            0.1754    0.0455   0.0087  0.0046     1.9    19.2     3345
Voynich v101 (words)           0.1847    0.0557   0.0102  0.0035     2.9    18.3     3353
Italian prose (words)          0.3538    0.1137   0.0659  0.0081     8.1    57.9     1652
Latin prose (words)            0.1391    0.0344   0.0117  0.0056     2.1    34.0     1652
five-component model           0.0877    0.0167   0.0040  0.0031     1.3    24.0     1782
self-citation (Timm)           0.1755    0.0675   0.0252  0.0065     3.9    37.4     1652

  `sigma` is the across-break value in units of its own surrogate
  spread. Below about 2 it is not distinguishable from zero.

  The Rohonc is written right to left and the transcription does not
  document its storage order, so both orientations are shown. Mutual
  information is symmetric, so `within` is identical either way; only
  the across-break pair changes, and both readings are reported.
