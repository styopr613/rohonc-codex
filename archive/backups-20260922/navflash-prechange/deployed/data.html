Data

The data is in the repository at https://github.com/SUP613/rohonc-codex. This page names the books the codex is read against and says where everything else goes.

The corpus

The books the codex is compiled from, which every folio is read against. What each supplied and where to read it is on The corpus.

  • King James Bible
  • Douay-Rheims Bible (Challoner revision)
  • Wake, The suppressed Gospels and Epistles of the original New Testament
  • York, Towneley, Chester and N-Town (Ludus Coventriae) play cycles
  • Caxton's Golden Legend
  • Barlaam and Ioasaph
  • The Roman Missal, English, 1865 and The Complete Office of Holy Week, 1875
  • R. H. Charles, The Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha of the Old Testament in English, vol. 2
  • Flavius Josephus, The Wars of the Jews

The outside review

The two reviewers' specifications are quoted whole: Gemini 2.5 Pro and Grok 4.7. Their replies as re-glossers and blind readers are files, and go up with the rest.

Everything else

The dictionary of added readings with its evidence, the folio English, the rendering, every saved run, the outside readers' replies, the working documents and the programs are posted to GitHub, with their history, so that nothing here links to a copy that can go stale. The standing orders are already published whole at The standing orders.

This rests on the Király and Tokai dictionary; their translation is unpublished; the readings here are this project's own.
